# Junk Magnet: store listing

**Title:** Junk Magnet

**Hook:** You're a magnet. Junk is your weapon.

**Short description (store card):**
Smash robots, steal their scrap and spin it into a planet-sized junk ball. Survive the swarm, beat three bosses, and crush The Compactor.

**Description:**
You're a living magnet. Every robot you break drops scrap that flies to you and orbits in
spinning rings, and that growing ball of junk is your weapon *and* your armour. Steer it into
the horde, Pulse to blast your junk outward, fire aimed fireballs, and pick a new upgrade every
level. Robots get angrier every phase, so keep moving: the Crusher arrives at 2:30, the
Magnetron at 4:30 and The Compactor at 6:00.

- **Your power is on screen:** the bigger your junk ball, the harder it hits, and you can watch it grow.
- **A different build every run:** 24 upgrades, 5 evolutions and four build paths (Fire, Storm, Tank, Shredder), plus slot-machine treasure chests.
- **Always one more run:** 6 magnets, 3 maps with their own hazards, a Daily Seed with a daily twist, challenges, achievements, and Hard and Nightmare modes.

**Controls:**
- Move: WASD / arrow keys (ZQSD on AZERTY) · drag anywhere on mobile
- Fireball: left-click aims at the cursor (auto-aims on mobile)
- Pulse: Space · Pulse button on mobile
- Specials: right-click or E · special button on mobile
- Pause: P · pause icon on mobile

**Rating:** PEGI 12-safe. Robots pop apart into junk, no blood.
