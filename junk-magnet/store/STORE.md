# Junk Magnet: store listing

**Title:** Junk Magnet

**Pitch:** Smash robots, steal their scrap, become a planet-sized junk ball.

**Description:**
You are a living magnet. Every robot you break drops scrap that flies to you and orbits in
spinning rings, and that growing ball of junk is your weapon *and* your armour. Steer it
into the robot horde, Pulse to blast your junk outward, pick an upgrade every level, and
survive until The Compactor arrives at 6:00.

- **Your power is on screen:** the bigger your junk ball, the harder it hits.
- **Pick 1 of 3 upgrades every level:** fire, explosive cans, counter-rotating rings, and evolutions.
- **Unlock magnets and maps that change how you play:** Fridge Magnet, Electromagnet, Black Hole, Factory conveyors and low-gravity Space Station, plus a Daily Seed.

**Controls:**
- Move: WASD / arrow keys (ZQSD on AZERTY) · drag anywhere on mobile
- Pulse: Space · Pulse button on mobile
- Pause: P · pause icon on mobile
