# Mycelion: store listing

**Title:** Mycelion (optional subtitle: *Hive Rising*)

**Hook:** They came to conquer your planet. Turn their army into your swarm.

**Short description (store card):**
You're the living heart of an alien world. Every invader you kill joins your swarm. Grow it, evolve it, and bring down the Colony Ship.

**Description:**
You're the Hive Core, the living heart of the alien world Mycelion. Human colonists drop from
orbit to take your planet, and every one you kill is infected and joins the swarm circling you.
That growing swarm is your weapon *and* your armour. Steer it into the invaders, unleash a Spore
Burst, spit acid, and evolve a new mutation every level. The colonists get angrier every phase,
so keep moving: the Siege Walker lands at 2:30, the Drop Carrier at 4:30 and The Colony Ship at 6:00.

- **Your power is on screen:** the bigger your swarm, the harder it hits, and you can watch it grow.
- **A different build every run:** 24 mutations, 6 evolutions and four build paths (Acid, Storm, Tank, Razor), plus slot-machine supply crates.
- **Always one more run:** 6 hive cores, 3 worlds (Mycelion, Vulkara, Cryonis) with lava vents and thorn walls, a Daily Seed with a daily twist, challenges, achievements, and Hard and Nightmare modes.

**Controls:**
- Move: WASD / arrow keys (ZQSD on AZERTY) · drag anywhere on mobile
- Acid Spit: left-click aims at the cursor (auto-aims on mobile)
- Spore Burst: Space · Spore Burst button on mobile
- Specials: right-click or E · special button on mobile
- Pause: P · pause icon on mobile

swarmlings; no blood, no gore.
